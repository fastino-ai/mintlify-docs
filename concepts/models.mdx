## Trainable models

Call `GET /v1/base-models?supports_training=true` for the current trainable
models and their capabilities. The endpoint is the source of truth for
availability.

### Encoders

Train these with a managed [`config` job](/api-reference/training-jobs/create)
and an uploaded dataset:

| Model | Training price |
| --- | --- |
| **GLiNER2 Base**<br />`fastino/gliner2-base-v1` | \$0.07 / GPU-minute<br />\$4.20 / GPU-hour |
| **GLiNER-2.5-Decide**<br />`fastino/GLiNER-2.5-Decide` | \$0.07 / GPU-minute<br />\$4.20 / GPU-hour |

### Decoders

Train these step by step with an application-controlled
[`interactive` job](/api-reference/training-jobs/interactive/create-model):

Interactive-training context limits are operation-specific capabilities. They
can differ from a base model's catalog `context_window` and from an inference
endpoint's request limit.

| Model | Callable ID | Prefill, not cached / 1M | Prefill, cached / 1M | Sample / 1M | Train / 1M |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nemotron 3.5 Super VL | `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Super-120B-A12B-BF16` | \$0.16 | \$0.032 | \$0.65 | \$0.65 |
| Nemotron 3.5 Lightning | `nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16` | \$0.05 | \$0.01 | \$0.35 | \$0.30 |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B | `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` | \$0.08 | \$0.016 | \$0.44 | \$0.44 |

“Nemotron 3.5 Super VL” is the product display name; use the callable ID shown
in the table in training requests. Interactive checkpoint storage is \$0.05
per GB-month.

Downloaded decoder adapters use Fastino's training-cell layout and are not
currently compatible with stock PEFT or standard vLLM. Sample them through the
Training API, or deploy a sampler checkpoint when its base model supports
deployment.

See the [Training API](/training) to choose a workflow and start a job.
