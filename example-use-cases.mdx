Use this map to find places where a structured, probabilistic decision can replace
a brittle prompt or manual review step. Open the closest workflow, adapt the example
to your own policy and data, then use the [GLiDE Reference](/concepts/decision-models)
for the request contract.

These are decision patterns, not built-in end-to-end workflows. Your application
supplies one case and owns the downstream action. Ask an independent question for
each property that can coexist. To rank a collection, score each item separately
and sort the results in application code.

## AI agents and automation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent action review" icon="key-round">
    - Decide whether a proposed tool call is allowed under the current policy.
    - Route an action to execute, revise, request approval, or stop.
    - Score the risk of sending an email, changing data, spending money, or publishing content.
    - Check whether the visible evidence supports the agent's stated reason for acting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Model and workflow routing" icon="sparkle">
    - Choose the best model, agent, tool, or workflow for each request.
    - Classify intent, domain, complexity, and risk before selecting a route.
    - Decide whether a request needs a specialist or more capable fallback.
    - Route uncertain cases to a human instead of forcing an automated path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI output verification" icon="file-lines">
    - Check whether a response follows the user's instructions and required format.
    - Evaluate whether supplied evidence supports a claim or citation.
    - Check each required step and detect unsupported conclusions or source conflicts.
    - Score an output against a product-specific quality rubric.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Context selection" icon="map">
    - Decide whether a passage is relevant enough to include in an AI context window.
    - Score query-to-document relevance before generation.
    - Detect conflicting or stale context that should be excluded.
    - Score candidate records individually, then sort them for retrieval or review.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Customer and revenue operations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customer support" icon="circle-help">
    - Route tickets by product area, issue type, account tier, or customer intent.
    - Score urgency, frustration, churn risk, or operational impact.
    - Check refund, replacement, or escalation eligibility against a supplied policy.
    - Verify that a proposed support response addresses the request and follows policy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sales and lead qualification" icon="badge-dollar-sign">
    - Evaluate whether an account matches an ideal customer profile.
    - Classify purchase intent, use case, company maturity, or sales motion.
    - Score fit, urgency, and likelihood of near-term engagement.
    - Route leads to self-serve, sales, partnerships, or nurture workflows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="E-commerce and marketplaces" icon="table-list">
    - Classify listings into the correct catalog or review queue.
    - Check whether a listing violates marketplace or advertising policy.
    - Score counterfeit, review-abuse, or seller-risk indicators.
    - Route uncertain listings to a specialist without blocking the full catalog.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trust, safety, and compliance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Content moderation" icon="circle-alert">
    - Check whether text violates a supplied rule for harassment, spam, fraud, or unsafe advice.
    - Classify the primary policy category for a moderation case.
    - Score severity and decide whether to allow, warn, review, or block.
    - Detect opt-out requests, personal-data exposure, or regulated claims.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prompt and tool security" icon="key-round">
    - Detect prompt injection, jailbreak attempts, or requests to expose private data.
    - Check whether a tool call exceeds the user's authorization or the agent's scope.
    - Score the risk of retrieved instructions before adding them to agent context.
    - Route suspicious activity to logging, isolation, or human review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Legal and policy review" icon="book-open">
    - Check each required provision in a contract, policy, filing, or marketing claim.
    - Detect prohibited claims, missing disclosures, or policy conflicts.
    - Classify documents by review type, jurisdiction, or responsible team.
    - Prioritize uncertain or high-risk items for qualified review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Financial and insurance operations" icon="database-zap">
    - Triage transaction narratives, claim notes, KYC records, or incident reports.
    - Classify risk type, claim complexity, or missing-information status.
    - Score fraud indicators, evidence quality, or review priority.
    - Route ambiguous cases to an investigator, adjuster, or compliance specialist.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Research, data, and product

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Scientific and qualitative research" icon="book-open">
    - Screen papers against explicit inclusion and exclusion criteria.
    - Check whether a passage supports a research claim.
    - Check separately for required controls, dataset details, and methodological information.
    - Check each predefined theme against interview and survey passages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product feedback and forecasting signals" icon="gauge">
    - Classify feedback by product area, request type, or customer segment.
    - Detect purchase intent, competitive pressure, supply concerns, or churn signals.
    - Score sentiment intensity, urgency, and likely business impact.
    - Turn probabilities into features for a forecasting or prioritization model.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Knowledge graph quality" icon="blocks">
    - Classify a proposed relationship between known entities.
    - Check whether a claim contradicts an existing record.
    - Score confidence in a candidate edge before adding it to a graph.
    - Route ambiguous relationships to a verification workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Semantic quality checks" icon="braces">
    - Check generated code against team-specific architectural conventions.
    - Detect whether a pull request is missing tests, migration steps, or documentation.
    - Score a document against a writing, accessibility, or brand rubric.
    - Run lightweight semantic checks in CI before requesting human review.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  Use GLiDE as decision support, not as the sole authority for irreversible or
  high-impact decisions involving safety, legal rights, employment, credit,
  healthcare, or access control. Validate thresholds on representative data and
  provide a human review path.
</Warning>

GLiDE evaluates supplied context; it does not extract text spans or generate prose.
Use [GLiNER](/concepts/gliner) for entity and relationship extraction, and a
generative model for open-ended writing.
