Use Fastino Agent to autonomously fine-tune for GLiNER and NVIDIA Nemotron models for your specific use cases. Deploy and inference both base and specialized fine-tuned models via our API.

## What you can do with Fastino

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Drop in, inference like normal" icon="plug" href="/concepts/inference">
    Point your existing OpenAI or Anthropic client at Fastino to run inference on GLiNER and Nemotron base models. 
  </Card>

  <Card title="Specialist model training" icon="brain" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Fastino trains and evaluates Nemotron 3.5 Lightning and GLiNER specialist models on your behalf. Zero MLOps from you.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bring your own evals" icon="bolt" href="/guides/fine-tune-llm">
    Use Fastino's built-in evaluation suite or plug in your own. Every training run also generates a full PDF report with eval details, rollout stages, and checkpoint history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Download your assets" icon="download" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Your weights and training datasets are yours. Download them at any time and bring them to any other platform or fine-tune further on your own infrastructure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported model families

- **GLiNER 2.5 Multilingual** for multilingual structured extraction.
- **GLiNER2 and GLiGuard** for entity extraction, classification, structured data, safety, and PII workflows.
- **Nemotron 3.5 Lightning** in general-purpose, Healthcare, and Finance variants.
