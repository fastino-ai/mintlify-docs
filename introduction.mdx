Point your OpenAI or Anthropic client at Fastino. We identify where your current model falls short, then train specialist models that are more accurate, cheaper, and faster.

## What you can do with Fastino

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Drop in, inference like normal" icon="plug" href="/concepts/inference">
    Point your existing OpenAI or Anthropic client at Fastino — same API, same code. No migration required.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Automatic gap detection" icon="chart-bar" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Fastino clusters your traffic by use case and surfaces exactly where your current model is leaving accuracy, cost, or latency on the table.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Specialist model training" icon="brain" href="/guides/fine-tune-llm">
    Fastino trains and evaluates Nemotron 3.5 Lightning and GLiNER specialist models on your behalf. Zero MLOps from you.
  </Card>

  <Card title="You control routing" icon="bolt" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Fastino surfaces lift, cost, and latency data for each specialist model. You decide when and how to route traffic to them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Your model retrains itself while you sleep.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Adaptive Inference" icon="rotate" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Fastino's continuous improvement loop. Mines your live production failures for high-signal examples, retrains a specialist model automatically, and promotes improved checkpoints behind the same endpoint. No redeployment required.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Download your assets" icon="download" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Your weights and training datasets are yours. Download them at any time — bring them to any other platform or fine-tune further on your own infrastructure.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Bring your own evals" icon="chart-bar" href="/concepts/evaluations">
    Use Fastino's built-in evaluation suite or plug in your own. Every retraining run is benchmarked before any traffic is promoted.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Full audit trail" icon="file-text" href="/guides/adaptive-inference">
    Every Adaptive Inference run generates a full PDF report — training data, eval deltas, rollout stages, and checkpoint history included.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported model families

- **GLiNER 2.5 Multilingual** for multilingual structured extraction.
- **GLiNER2 and GLiGuard** for entity extraction, classification, structured data, safety, and PII workflows.
- **Nemotron 3.5 Lightning** in general-purpose, Healthcare, and Finance variants.

See [Models](/concepts/models) for model IDs and current availability.
