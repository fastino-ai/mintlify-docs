Use Fastino to fine-tune GLiNER models for your specific use cases. Run inference with both base and specialized fine-tuned models through our API.

## What you can do with Fastino

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Training API" icon="database-zap" href="/training">
    Fine-tune GLiNER models for your specific use cases through Fastino's Training API.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Inference API" icon="sparkle" href="/inference">
    Run inference with base and specialized fine-tuned models through Fastino's Inference API.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported model families

- **GLiNER 2.5 Multilingual** for multilingual structured extraction.
- **GLiNER2 and GLiGuard** for entity extraction, classification, structured data, safety, and PII workflows.

See [Models](/concepts/models) for model IDs and current availability.
