**GLiDE** is Fastino's decision model: it answers classification, routing, and scoring questions with calibrated probabilities instead of generated text. Reach for **GLiNER** when you need span-level extraction instead, and fine-tune either family on your own data through Fastino's Training API.

## Supported model families

- **GLiDE** for decisions: classification, routing, and scoring with calibrated probabilities instead of generated text.
- **GLiNER-2.5-Decide** for typed decisions, constraints, span extraction, relations, and structured records.
- **GLiNER 2.5 Multilingual** for multilingual structured extraction.
- **GLiNER2 and GLiGuard** for entity extraction, classification, structured data, safety, and PII workflows.

See [Models](/concepts/models) for model IDs and current availability.
