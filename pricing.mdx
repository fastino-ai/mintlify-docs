Pricing for inference and training GLiNER models.

- Inference: \$0.03 per 1M input tokens / \$0 output
- Training: \$0.07 per minute (\$4.20 per hour)

Credit top-ups are available as you continue using Fastino, so you can keep your inference and training workflows running.
