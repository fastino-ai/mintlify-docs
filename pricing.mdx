## Training

Config jobs are billed by GPU time. Interactive jobs are billed by token.

### Config jobs

\$0.07 per GPU-minute (\$4.20 per GPU-hour) to fine-tune GLiNER models with a
managed config job.

### Interactive jobs

Sample and Train prices are USD per 1M tokens.

| Model | Sample | Train |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nemotron 3.5 Super VL<br />**Limited-time discount** | ~~\$0.96~~ \$0.65 | ~~\$0.72~~ \$0.65 |
| Nemotron 3.5 Lightning | \$0.35<br />29% below Tinker ↓ | \$0.30<br />32% below Tinker ↓ |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B | \$0.44<br />67% below Tinker ↓ | \$0.44<br />63% below Tinker ↓ |

Train covers forward and forward/backward tokens. Sample covers sampling usage.

GLiDE responses report `usage.output_tokens` for their structured answers, but those output
tokens are priced at \$0; only input tokens are billed at the listed rate.

See the [Model Catalog](/concepts/models) for pricing alongside each model ID.

Credit top-ups are available as you continue using Fastino, so you can keep your inference and training workflows running.
