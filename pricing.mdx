GLiDE responses report `usage.output_tokens` for their structured answers, but those output
tokens are priced at \$0; only input tokens are billed at the listed rate.

See the [Model Catalog](/concepts/models) for pricing alongside each model ID.

## Training

### Config jobs

\$0.07 per GPU-minute (\$4.20 per GPU-hour) to fine-tune GLiNER models with a
managed config job.

### Interactive jobs

Token prices depend on the model and operation. Prices are per 1M tokens.

| Model | Training | Sampling input | Cached sampling input | Sampling output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nemotron 3 Super<br />`nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Super-120B-A12B-BF16` | \$0.96 | \$0.16 | \$0.032 | \$0.72 |
| Nemotron 3.5 Lightning<br />`nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16` | \$0.30 | \$0.05 | \$0.01 | \$0.35 |

Training covers forward and forward/backward tokens. Sampling is billed
separately for input, cached input, and output tokens.

Credit top-ups are available as you continue using Fastino, so you can keep your inference and training workflows running.
