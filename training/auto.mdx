Auto Train is Fastino's private-access autonomous training agent. You
provide the training objective and success criteria. The agent plans and runs
the training lifecycle.

The agent can coordinate:

- Dataset preparation and validation
- Candidate training and evaluation
- Questions and approval checkpoints
- Progress against a measurable success criterion
- Delivery of models that meet the approved bar

<Note>
  Auto Train is in private access and is not included in the public OpenAPI
  or API reference.
</Note>

Auto Train has no public endpoints or request schemas.
