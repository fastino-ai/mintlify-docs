Each cookbook is a working path for a common task. Copy a cookbook, swap in your own data, and adjust its criteria, labels, or reward.

The GLiNER cookbooks cover the three most common ways teams use GLiNER 2.5 Multi: entity extraction, classification, and relation extraction.
